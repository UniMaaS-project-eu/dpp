About UniMaaS

Unified Modeling and Automated Scheduling for Manufacturing as a Service

UniMaaS vision is to deliver a platform for flexible, agile and decentralized manufacturing, embracing the Manufacturing as a Service (MaaS) paradigm.

This Digital Product Passport (DPP) system enables comprehensive tracking and management of products throughout their lifecycle, providing transparency and traceability in manufacturing processes.

Key features:

  • Product registration and lifecycle tracking
  • Comprehensive product information management
  • Manufacturing process transparency
  • Integration with blockchain technology
  • Support for Industry 4.0 standards
